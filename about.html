Why I built PoolChem Tracker

In 2022, my family built a house and then built an inground saltwater pool from scratch.

I knew nothing about pool chemistry when the water went in. But I like numbers and spreadsheets, so from the first week I started logging every test result from the pool store.

After a few months, patterns started to emerge, and they did not match what I was being told. The pool store kept recommending products that did not seem to move the needle. I tried every pool app I could find. None of them helped. I was still getting cloudy water, still adding chemicals, still not understanding why.

Then I figured out what was actually going on: I live in a desert climate. High heat, fast evaporation, hard fill water, intense sun. The problems I was fighting (climbing stabilizer levels, aggressive chlorine demand, scale buildup) were directly tied to where I lived. And neither the pool store advice nor any of the apps I tried accounted for any of that.

Scale was one of the worst issues, and tracking it down led me to do a deep dive into the Langelier Saturation Index (LSI), a calculation that measures whether water is balanced, corrosive, or prone to scaling. It turned out to be the piece most apps were skipping entirely.

I decided there had to be a better way. So I built one.

That spreadsheet became PoolChem Tracker. I wanted something that tracked readings over time, spotted trends before they became problems, and gave recommendations that reflected my actual pool and my actual climate, not a generic average.

Who it is for

PoolChem Tracker is for people who want to understand their pool instead of constantly reacting to problems.

Whether you are opening your first pool, maintaining a saltwater system, or have been caring for pools for years, the goal is the same: clearer water, fewer surprises, and less wasted money.

You do not need to understand every chemistry term to use the app. PoolChem Tracker handles the calculations, tracks trends over time, and explains what matters in plain English.

Why privacy matters

I never liked the idea of creating an account just to use a simple tool.

When I built PoolChem Tracker, I decided to keep things as straightforward as possible. The app stores your pool data on your device and works without an internet connection.

That means no accounts, no cloud storage, and no tracking.

Your pool data belongs to you, and it stays with you.

The principles behind PoolChem Tracker

🎯 Solve real problems

Every feature exists for one reason: to help you understand or improve your pool water. No social feed. No gamification. No unnecessary complexity.

🔒 Privacy by default

Your readings stay on your device. I cannot see your data because I never collect it.

💰 Honest pricing

The app is free to try and costs $4.99 to unlock everything. No subscriptions, no ads, and no recurring charges.

🧪 Accuracy matters

The app calculates the Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) to detect scale and corrosion risk, enforces minimum chlorine targets based on your stabilizer (CYA) level, and adjusts recommendations for your pool type, temperature, and whether you run a salt system.
