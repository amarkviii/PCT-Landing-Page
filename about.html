Why I built this app

When I bought my house with a pool, I realized pretty quickly that I had no idea how to take care of it.

The test strips gave me numbers I didn't fully understand. The pool store always seemed to recommend buying more chemicals. YouTube videos all contradicted each other. I was spending money, pouring things into the water, and somehow still ending up with a cloudy pool.

Eventually, I started logging all my readings in a spreadsheet just to make sense of it all. Once I could actually see the trends over time, things finally started to click. I learned that the order of adjustments matters. Alkalinity before pH. I learned how stabilizer (CYA) slowly creeps up when using tablets. And I discovered that LSI is the number that really tells you whether your water is balanced.

PoolChem Tracker grew out of that experience.

I wanted something simpler and smarter than my spreadsheet. An app that could calculate exact chemical doses, help me understand what was happening in the water, and show trends over time. All without needing a chemistry background or an internet connection.

Who it's for

I built PoolChem Tracker for people like me. Pool owners who want to actually understand their water instead of guessing and throwing chemicals at problems.

Whether you're opening your first pool or you've maintained one for years, the app helps you make clearer decisions with less waste, less frustration, and fewer trips to the pool store. It's free to start, so you can see if it works for you before committing.

It works with traditional chlorine pools and salt water systems. And you don't need to know what "LSI" means to use it. The app handles the calculations and explains what matters in plain English.

Why it's private

One thing that mattered to me from the beginning was privacy.

Your pool data shouldn't require an account, a subscription, or a cloud server just to work. That's why PoolChem Tracker stores everything directly on your device. No sign-ups. No analytics tracking. No internet required.

The app works completely offline because your data should stay with you. Simple as that.
