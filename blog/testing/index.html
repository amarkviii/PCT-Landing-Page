Pool water testing guides

The quality of your pool chemistry decisions is only as good as the quality of your test results. A bad sample, a test strip used in full sun, or a reagent kit that's been open since last summer can give you numbers that look fine while your water is quietly out of balance. These guides cover how to collect a reliable sample, when test strips are accurate enough and when you need a liquid kit, the best time of day to test for the most accurate FC and pH readings, and how often to test based on what's actually changing in your water.
