Cloudy pool water guides

Cloudy pool water almost always comes down to one of five causes: low chlorine, high pH, high alkalinity, poor filtration, or high calcium. The trick is diagnosing the right one before reaching for a clarifier — treating the symptom without fixing the cause just kicks the problem down the road. These guides walk through the diagnosis step by step, the 24-hour clearing protocol when you need it fast, and what to do in the frustrating post-shock window when the water looks worse before it gets better.

Pool Cloudy After Adding Chemicals?

Brief cloudiness after adding CYA, calcium chloride, or soda ash is normal and clears in under 2 hours. Cloudiness that persists past 24 hours means pH or calcium is out of balance.
