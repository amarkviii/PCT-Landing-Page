Website Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 14, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 14, 2026

This policy covers the PoolChem Tracker website only. The mobile app is governed by a separate Privacy Policy.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the PoolChem Tracker website collects and processes information when you visit or use the website, including blog content and interactive calculators.

Information Automatically Processed

When you visit this website, certain technical information may be processed automatically by our hosting and service providers, including Cloudflare and analytics providers. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and navigation patterns
  • Time and date of access
  • Referring URLs

This information is used for website operation, security, performance monitoring, and analytics.

Analytics Services

This website uses third-party analytics services to understand usage and improve content and performance. We use:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC)
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation)

These services may collect and process information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, clicks, scroll behavior, interaction patterns, device and browser information, approximate geographic location, and IP address (which may be anonymized depending on settings).

These providers may use cookies or similar technologies. Data collected by these services is processed in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Cookies

Third-party analytics services used on this website may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality or analytics accuracy.

Blog Content

The website includes informational blog posts related to pool chemistry, maintenance, and water treatment. No account or registration is required to access this content.

Calculators and Tools

The website provides interactive pool chemistry calculators and similar tools. Where possible, calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not stored or transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, which may process limited technical information such as IP addresses and request data for security, performance, and delivery purposes.

We may also embed third-party content (such as videos or external tools), which may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
