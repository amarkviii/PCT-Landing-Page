Frequently asked questions

Which pool calculator should I use first?

Start with the pool volume calculator. Every dose calculator on this page — chlorine, shock, muriatic acid, baking soda, calcium hardness, and salt — needs your pool's gallon count as an input. Find your volume once and the rest of the math follows.

How do I calculate how much chlorine to add to my pool?

Use the chlorine dose calculator. Enter your pool volume, current free chlorine (FC) reading, and target FC. The calculator supports liquid chlorine (10% and 12.5% sodium hypochlorite), calcium hypochlorite (cal-hypo 65% and 73%), and trichlor tablets.

How much shock does my pool need?

Shock level is CYA × 40% — it is not a fixed number. At CYA 50 ppm, shock level is 20 ppm FC. At CYA 80, it is 32 ppm FC. The pool shock calculator computes the exact dose for your pool volume and CYA.

How much muriatic acid do I need to lower pool pH?

Use the muriatic acid calculator. Enter your pool volume, current pH, and target pH (7.4–7.6 is the standard range). The calculator supports 31.45% full-strength and 14.5% diluted muriatic acid, and also handles alkalinity reduction.

Are these pool calculators free?

Yes — all calculators on this page are completely free to use. No account, no sign-up, and no app required.
